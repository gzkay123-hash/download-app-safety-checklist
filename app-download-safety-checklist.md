# App Download Safety Checklist

Use this checklist before installing an Android or iOS app.

## 1. Confirm the official source

- Prefer Google Play, App Store, or the developer's official website.
- Check the developer name, icon, screenshots, and official domain.
- Avoid random APK mirrors unless you can verify the package is official.

Reference: https://downloadappguide.com/news/how-to-check-apk-official-source-before-install

## 2. Check region and device compatibility

If an app is unavailable, confirm account country, device model, OS version, age rating, and the developer's official announcement.

Reference: https://downloadappguide.com/news/google-play-app-not-available-region-fix

## 3. Review permissions

Pay extra attention to camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, notification, accessibility, and background activity permissions.

Reference: https://downloadappguide.com/news/android-app-permission-risk-level-guide

## 4. Avoid cracked or modified apps

Cracked or modded apps can expose accounts, payments, private files, and device security.

Reference: https://downloadappguide.com/news/why-avoid-cracked-modded-apps
