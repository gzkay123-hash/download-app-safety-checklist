# App Download Safety Checklist

Use this checklist before installing an app from outside an official store.

## Before downloading

- Confirm the app's official developer or publisher.
- Search for the official store listing first.
- Compare icon, package name, version, and publisher name.
- Review permission requests, especially SMS, accessibility, contacts, wallet, VPN, and notification access.
- Avoid cracked/modded builds for finance, messaging, wallet, social, or work accounts.
